# AI Search Page Audit

Use this checklist on a single page or template family.

## Access
- [ ] Canonical URL resolves successfully.
- [ ] Crawl directives match the intended access policy.
- [ ] Important content is present in the rendered document.
- [ ] Canonical and language annotations are internally consistent.

## Retrieval and understanding
- [ ] The page has a specific information task.
- [ ] Definitions and claims retain their scope and qualifiers.
- [ ] Headings expose the information hierarchy.
- [ ] Entity names and relationships are unambiguous.

## Evidence and citability
- [ ] Important claims identify primary evidence where available.
- [ ] Vendor claims, observations and author synthesis are distinguishable.
- [ ] Dates and measurement windows are explicit where material.
- [ ] Quotable facts do not depend on missing context.

## Measurement
- [ ] Availability, visibility, engagement and outcome are measured separately.
- [ ] Mention, citation, referral and conversion are separate observations.
- [ ] Engine, market, language and observation date are recorded.
