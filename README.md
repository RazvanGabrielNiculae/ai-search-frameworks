# AI Search Frameworks

Open frameworks and practical methodology for SEO, AEO, GEO and AI Search.

Maintained by **Razvan Niculae**. Research notes, detailed guides and source articles are published at [niculae.info](https://niculae.info/).

## Core model

AI-search visibility is easier to reason about as a connected system rather than as separate optimization silos:

**Crawlability → Retrieval → Answerability → Citability → Measurement**

- **SEO** provides the technical and discovery foundation.
- **AEO** makes useful answers explicit, scoped and easier to extract.
- **GEO** focuses on whether information can be retrieved, understood, verified and cited in AI-mediated experiences.
- **Measurement** separates observable visibility from engagement and business outcomes.

Detailed source: [GEO vs AEO: definitions, overlap and practical boundaries](https://niculae.info/blog/geo-vs-aeo/)

## Operating framework

A second model connects implementation to outcomes:

**Discover → Retrieve → Understand → Cite → Convert**

A page should first be discoverable and accessible, then retrievable for a real task, understandable without losing important context, supported well enough to cite, and finally connected to a useful next action.

Implementation guide: [SEO + GEO implementation framework](https://niculae.info/blog/seo-geo-implementation-framework/)

## SEO and AI Search

AI Search does not replace the fundamentals of technical SEO, information architecture, useful content and clear source identity. It adds retrieval, synthesis, citation and platform-specific access considerations.

Reference: [AI SEO vs traditional SEO](https://niculae.info/blog/ai-seo-vs-traditional-seo/)

## Information gain

Useful information gain comes from new evidence, synthesis, examples, decision boundaries or practical utility — not from paraphrasing what already exists.

When documenting claims, distinguish between:

- primary facts and primary documentation;
- vendor or third-party claims;
- first-party observations;
- author synthesis and interpretation.

Reference: [Information gain for SEO and AI Search](https://niculae.info/blog/information-gain-seo/)

## Entity clarity

Entity optimization is primarily an identity and consistency problem. Names, canonical URLs, descriptions, authorship and relationships should converge on stable facts across owned properties and trustworthy external sources. Structured data should represent those facts, not attempt to repair contradictory identity.

Reference: [Practical entity SEO guide](https://niculae.info/blog/entity-seo-practical-guide/)

## Measurement principles

Keep these layers separate:

**Availability → Visibility → Engagement → Outcome**

And do not collapse different observations into a single vanity metric:

**Mention ≠ Citation ≠ Referral ≠ Conversion**

**Citation frequency ≠ Citation coverage**

For every metric, define the source or engine, observation window, cohort or denominator where applicable, geography, known blind spots and evidence location.

## Repository principles

This repository is topic-first and evidence-first. Assets should be useful even without an outbound link. It is not intended for backlink automation, duplicate-content blasting, artificial profiles, exact-match anchor schemes or unsupported ranking claims.

Future additions may include diagrams, checklists, measurement templates and reference datasets derived from the frameworks above.
